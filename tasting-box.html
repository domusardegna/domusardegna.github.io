How to taste it

Our suggestions

Tasting order

Start with the fresh, move on to the semi-aged and finish with the aged: from mild to bold.

Before serving

Take the cheese out of the fridge about 30 minutes before tasting.

With the honey

Add a drizzle of honey to the aged pecorino.

Pairings for the whole box

  • Sourdough bread or crackers
  • Fresh figs or grapes
  • Fig jam or quince paste (membrillo)
  • Prosciutto or salami
  • Toasted almonds and green olives
  • Medium-bodied reds such as Chianti, Montepulciano or Cannonau, or crisp whites such as Vermentino
