Pecorino tenero 3 mesi

Aged 3 months · 250 g

The cheese

Based on the producer's description

A soft, young sheep's-milk pecorino with a delicate, aromatic flavour. Versatile and perfect for the whole family.

Origin

The pastoral areas of Sardinia (Barbagia, Marmilla, Ogliastra and Logudoro), from Sardinian sheep raised on free pasture rich in wild herbs and Mediterranean scrub.

Milk & aging

Whole sheep's milk, matured for about 3 months (90 days).

Texture

Soft, elastic and still moist, with a thin natural rind.

Taste

Sweet and delicate, slightly milky and lightly salty, with a harmonious, gentle finish.

Aroma

Fresh and milky, with light herbaceous notes.

In the kitchen

Fresh, light cheese boards, sandwiches, starters and aperitifs. Melts well on pizza, pasta and vegetables, and works in summer salads and creamy recipes.

Pairings

Food and wine: the producer's recommendations

Food

  • Pane carasau (Sardinian flatbread), civraxiu (Sardinian bread) or soft rolls
  • Young Sardinian cured meats: prosciutto crudo, pancetta, sweet sausage
  • Citrus or wildflower honey, or pear or fig jam
  • Grilled courgettes, aubergines and peppers
  • Fresh tomatoes, olives and summer salads

Wine & drinks

  • Fresh Sardinian whites: Vermentino, Nuragus, Semidano
  • Mineral, fruity rosé wines
  • Light reds: Monica, young Cannonau
  • Pale or unfiltered craft beers, or a dry sparkling wine

With Danish favourites

Our own suggestions
  • On rugbrød (Danish rye bread) or as a smørrebrød (open sandwich) topping with cucumber and radish
  • With fresh Danish apples or pears
  • With elderflower (hyldeblomst) or rhubarb jam
  • With a Danish pilsner, wheat beer or dry apple cider
