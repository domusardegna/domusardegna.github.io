Pecorino tenero 3 mesi

Aged 3 months · 250 g

The cheese

Translated from the producer's description

Soft pecorino aged 3 months, with a delicate and aromatic flavour. Soft and elastic paste, perfect for cheese boards, sandwiches, starters and everyday cooking.

📍 Origin

Produced in Sardinia, in the pastoral areas of Barbagia, Marmilla, Ogliastra and Logudoro, where making pecorino is an art handed down through generations.

The milk comes from Sardinian sheep raised on free pasture, rich in wild herbs and Mediterranean scrub.

🧀 What it is

Pecorino tenero 3 mesi is a short-aged sheep's cheese, made from whole sheep's milk.

After about 90 days of maturing, it has:

  • a soft, elastic and still moist paste,
  • a thin, natural rind,
  • a sweet, slightly milky and pleasant flavour.

It is a versatile cheese, perfect for the whole family.

📍 Provenienza

Prodotto in Sardegna, nelle aree pastorali di Barbagia, Marmilla, Ogliastra e Logudoro, dove la lavorazione del pecorino è un’arte tramandata da generazioni.

Il latte proviene da pecore sarde allevate al pascolo libero, ricco di erbe spontanee e macchia mediterranea.

🧀 Cos’è

Il pecorino tenero 3 mesi è un formaggio ovino a breve stagionatura, ottenuto da latte intero di pecora.

Dopo circa 90 giorni di maturazione, presenta:

  • una pasta morbida, elastica e ancora umida,
  • una crosta sottile e naturale,
  • un sapore dolce, leggermente lattico e piacevole.

È un formaggio versatile, perfetto per tutta la famiglia.

Pairings

From the producer's recommended pairings

Food

  • Pane Carasau, Civraxiu or soft bread rolls
  • Young Sardinian cured meats: prosciutto crudo, pancetta, sweet sausage
  • Citrus or wildflower honey, or pear/fig jam
  • Grilled vegetables, courgettes, aubergines, peppers
  • Fresh tomatoes, olives, summer salads
  • Excellent melted on pizzas, focaccias or bruschetta

Wine & drinks

  • Fresh white wines: Vermentino, Nuragus, Semidano
  • Mineral and fruity rosé wines
  • Light red wines: Monica, young Cannonau
  • Pale or unfiltered craft beers
  • Also perfect with dry sparkling wines
