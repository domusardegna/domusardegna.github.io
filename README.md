# DOMU — Cheese from the heart of Sardinia

DOMU'S RECIPES is a small home-based selection of Sardinian sheep's-milk cheese (pecorino), honey and olive oil, based in Copenhagen.

## What's in this folder

- `index.html`: the home page. It has the nonna hero and 3 cheese cards; each card links to its own page. Images and font load from `assets/`.
- `pecorino-tenero.html`, `pecorino-semistagionato.html`, `pecorino-stagionato.html`: one page per cheese. The semi-aged page is San Platano by Picciau Formaggi. Each page has "The cheese" and "Pairings" side by side.
- `tasting-box.html`: the DOMU tasting box (the three cheeses + a 200 g jar of premium Sardinian organic honey), with "What's inside" and "How to taste it". Its images (`assets/tasting-box.jpg`, `assets/tasting-box-wide.jpg`) are collages. The fresh and aged panels are the current product photos. The semi-aged panel is the earlier studio photo; the San Platano photo is on the semi-aged card and product page.
- `domu-cheeses-standalone.html`: the same page with everything embedded (base64), so it works offline as a single file.
- `assets/`
  - Cheese photos: `pecorino-tenero-3-mesi.jpg`, `san-platano.jpg` (semi-aged, San Platano), `pecorino-stagionato-10-mesi.jpg`
  - San Platano spec sheets: `san-platano-spec-sheet.pdf` (English translation), `san-platano-scheda-tecnica-it.pdf` (Picciau's Italian original)
  - `hero-nonna.jpg`: the hero photo (AI-generated image)
  - `lockup-domus-recipes.png` / `.webp`: the "DOMU'S RECIPES" lettering with the sheep icon and subtitle, as on the poster
  - `sheep-icon.png`: the yellow sheep icon
  - `LondrinaSolid-Regular.ttf`: the heading font (Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License)
- `posters/`: the approved posters (`domu-nonna-home-1.jpg`, `domu-recipes-londrina.jpg`)
- `data/`: the cheese descriptions, line by line (`descriptions-it.json` is the original Italian, `descriptions-en.json` the English translation)
