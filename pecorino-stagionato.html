Pecorino stagionato 10 mesi

Aged 10 months · 250 g

The cheese

Based on the producer's description

An aged sheep's-milk pecorino with a bold but balanced flavour: a cheese with character, yet still mellow in its complexity.

Origin

The pastoral areas of Sardinia (Barbagia, Ogliastra, Marmilla and Logudoro), where Sardinian sheep graze freely on wild herbs, giving the milk a unique aromatic profile.

Milk & aging

Whole sheep's milk, given a long, harmonious maturing of 10 months.

Texture

Hard, compact and slightly crumbly, with a sturdy natural rind.

Taste

Intense, savoury and rich, with a light, pleasant sharpness and a long, harmonious finish.

Aroma

Bold and persistent, with herbaceous notes, hay and dried fruit and nuts.

In the kitchen

Aged-cheese boards, shavings with honey or jam, and gourmet starters and aperitifs. Ideal grated over first courses, rustic soups, malloreddus and culurgiones, and in traditional recipes.

Pairings

Food and wine: the producer's recommendations

Food

  • Pane carasau, civraxiu or pistoccu (Sardinian breads)
  • Sardinian cured meats: aged sausage, mustela, lonza (cured pork loins), prosciutto crudo
  • Chestnut or strawberry-tree honey, for contrast
  • Pear, fig or red onion jam
  • Grilled vegetables, sautéed mushrooms and artichokes

Wine & drinks

  • Full-bodied Sardinian reds: Cannonau, Carignano del Sulcis, Bovale, Mandrolisai
  • Full-bodied whites: Vermentino superiore, dry Nasco
  • Amber or strong ale craft beers
  • After dinner: filu 'e ferru (Sardinian grape spirit) or white mirto (Sardinian myrtle liqueur)

With Danish favourites

Our own suggestions
  • Thinly shaved on rugbrød (Danish rye bread) smørrebrød
  • With Danish heather (lyng) honey and walnuts
  • With baked or dried Danish apples, or a sharp rhubarb compote
  • With a dark Danish craft beer or a small glass of akvavit (Scandinavian caraway spirit)
