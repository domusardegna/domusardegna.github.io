San Platano

At least 120 days (about 4 months) · 250 g

Photo: Formaggi Picciau

Made by Formaggi Picciau

The cheese

Based on the producer's description

San Platano is an aged sheep's-milk cheese with an intense, enveloping aroma. On the palate it is full, savoury and persistent, in the Sardinian cheesemaking tradition. Ageing gives it a decisive but balanced character, for anyone who likes clear, recognisable flavours tied to the land.

Producer

Formaggi Picciau, Decimomannu (CA), Sardinia.

Ageing

At least 120 days (about 4 months).

Ingredients

Sheep's milk, rennet, salt and native cultures.

Allergens

Milk (lactose, milk proteins).

Rind

The rind is not edible. It is treated with extra virgin olive oil and vinegar.

Storage

Store at +8/10 °C.

Picciau's product page gives these ingredients, ageing of at least 120 days, and storage at +8/10 °C. The technical sheet (revision 00, 16/10/14) lists more detailed ingredients, ageing from 60 days, and storage from +2 to +10 °C. Those sheet figures are unchanged in the PDF.

Pairings

Our suggestions, easy to find anywhere

Food

  • Crusty bread or plain crackers
  • Dried figs, dates or quince paste (membrillo)
  • Dark chocolate (70% cocoa or more)
  • Spicy salami or chorizo
  • Grated over pasta: cacio e pepe, carbonara or amatriciana

Wine & drinks

  • Full-bodied reds such as Primitivo, Cannonau or Nero d'Avola
  • Rich reds such as Amarone or Valpolicella Ripasso
  • Tawny port or another fortified wine
  • A strong Belgian-style ale

With Danish favourites

Our own suggestions
  • Thinly shaved on rugbrød (Danish rye bread) smørrebrød
  • With Danish heather (lyng) honey and walnuts
  • With baked or dried Danish apples, or a sharp rhubarb compote
  • With a dark Danish craft beer or a small glass of akvavit (Scandinavian caraway spirit)
